Our Craft

What goes into every piece

01

Select the timber

We choose mature, responsibly sourced teak and mahogany, then kiln-dry it to the right moisture level.

02

Design & measure

Pick from our range or bring your own sketch, photo or architect's drawing. We plan it around your space.

03

Hand-crafted joinery

Our craftsmen use mortise-and-tenon and dovetail joints — strong, traditional and built to last.

04

Finish & deliver

Sanded and hand-polished in your chosen finish, then delivered and set up in your home.

About The Teak

Sri Lankan craftsmanship, made for modern homes.

The Teak began in a small family workshop with a simple belief: real wood, honestly made, only gets better with time. Today our craftsmen still cut, join and finish every piece by hand — pairing the timeless strength of Sri Lankan teak with clean, modern design.

  • Solid hardwood frames — never particleboard
  • Traditional mortise-and-tenon joinery
  • Finishes chosen for tropical humidity
  • Ready stock or made to order in 3–5 weeks
